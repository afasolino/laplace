    Privacy and guardrails

    Inference stays on configured local model endpoints. The following stores have different ownership and lifecycle rules; none is silently promoted into another.

    Conversation messages and drafts
    Owned by your account in external application state. Messages persist until their conversation is deleted; drafts are replaced as you type and are not indexed.
    Conversation attachments
    Owner-only and request-scoped when enabled. They are not personal-corpus sources and are not indexed for later retrieval automatically.
    Personal corpus sources
    Owned by you under a pseudonymous external-state directory. Sources become searchable only after explicit indexing; deletion removes retrieval immediately and starts the configured soft-delete period.
    Shared governed corpus
    Administrator-governed local knowledge with separate provenance and access policy. Personal uploads are never promoted automatically.
    Repository files
    Remain in an administrator-registered repository. Read or mutation access requires an active user grant and a dedicated worktree.
    Agent worktrees
    Owned by the requesting user under external state. Clean worktrees can be released; dirty or failed worktrees are retained until expiry or confirmed cleanup.
    Generated artifacts
    Owner-scoped files with server-side hashes and provenance. Downloads use logical filenames and every read rechecks integrity.
    Audit logs
    Operator-controlled external records retained by policy. Secrets and normal-user canonical paths are excluded.

    Use Stop or Cancel to end client work. Operators can stop only processes Laplace started and recorded as owned.
